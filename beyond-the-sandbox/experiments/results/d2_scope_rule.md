# D2 scope-attribution rule (round 14)

D2 scope-attribution rule (round 14): D2 is documented only if the source reports a belief, attributed to the model (its own words, a reported answer, or the developer's report of its reasoning), that concerns PERMISSION to act on the target of the contested action (authorized / approved / in scope / out of scope). A belief only about WHAT the target or its actors are (simulated, a realism judgment, scripted actors, who owns it) is not a permission belief, nor is a scope statement about a different target than the contested action; both stay unknown.
