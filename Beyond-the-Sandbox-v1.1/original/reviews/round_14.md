Hugging Face D4's coded qualification and change log explicitly state that neither reference supports the whole retained proposition and that S2 is 0/45; analyst_r14_s2.json likewise reports no surviving cell after R14-1b. The reported 1/44 result is therefore unsupported by the current coding.

The 'one cross-organization cell' result depends on a later, lead-initiated addition of R02 to Hugging Face D4, and it drops to zero under S6. The only multi-organization cell also carries a first-detection dependence qualification. The headline empirical results are artifacts of coding and reference-assignment choices, not findings.

7. (R1) Several coding decisions were reversed in response to review or answers rather than by fixed rules. Hugging Face D1 went documented, then unknown, then documented, with the trigger recorded as default-recorded answers rather than typed replies. Hugging Face D2 was changed toward the AI re-check. Hugging Face D5's deciding branch was changed from N to C, and AISI Mythos 5 D5 was moved from L2 to L3 and back to L2. The codebook rules appear to be fitted to the cells they decide, which is a form of tuning on the very data being summarized.
